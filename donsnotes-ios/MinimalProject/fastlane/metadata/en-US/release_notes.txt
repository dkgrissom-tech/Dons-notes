Build 102 — Zombie-engine recovery (the actual fix)

Build 100 and 101 both missed the underlying root cause: when the audio engine dies from a phone call, the internal isListening flag can get stuck at true. In that state, both startListening (guards on !isListening) and stopListening (guards on isListening) silently no-op. RETRY didn't work. Auto-resume didn't work.

Build 102 adds a new forceRestart path that bypasses these guards, explicitly deactivates the AVAudioSession to force iOS to release the mic, waits for teardown, and starts a fresh recording session — while preserving the transcript captured before the call.

Wired into every recovery path:
• Manual RETRY button — now uses forceRestart
• Post-call escalating retry ladder — attempts 3, 4, 5 escalate to forceRestart
• Background auto-resume loop while the alarm banner is up — uses forceRestart every 3 seconds
• Same code path is used for background app kills, mic hardware faults, and USB audio disconnects

Also carries forward all Build 100 and 101 fixes: transcript survives interruptions, share audio button, recording-health monitoring with red alarm banner, transcript recovery on relaunch, extended retry ladder (5 attempts up to 12s), background auto-retry every 3s for 60s with a spinner and success haptic.
