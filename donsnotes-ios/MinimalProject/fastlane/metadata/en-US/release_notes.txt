Build 105 — No more empty recap emails.

FIXED: Ora used to compose a recap email even when the recording captured zero speech, resulting in an email with just "Meeting Recap — ORA / Date / Attendees" and no summary, no transcript, no content. That silent-failure mode is gone.

NEW: After you stop recording, the upload screen now shows you a preview of exactly what Ora heard. You'll see the word count and the full captured transcript in a scrollable box BEFORE you tap "Upload & Process." If it looks wrong you can discard right there.

NEW: When a recording captures no audio, the upload screen replaces the "Upload & Process" button with a clear warning: "Ora didn't hear any speech." The only option is Discard. No hollow email will ever go out.

Belt-and-suspenders: even if some code path reaches uploadMeeting with empty content, it will now bail out cleanly instead of firing off an empty email.

Carries forward all Build 100-104 fixes: transcript recovery, share audio, force-restart zombie engine, diagnostic logging, no more banner-flicker, always-visible CONTINUE RECORDING button, background audio for app-switch survival.
