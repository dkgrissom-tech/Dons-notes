Build 110 — fix empty recap email caused by canceled recognizer callback.

Root cause found via Build 109's diagnostic log: transcript had 127 words / 589 chars at stopListening, then 0 chars 2.2 seconds later at uploadMeeting. The final SFSpeechRecognizer callback delivered after task cancellation carries an empty bestTranscription.formattedString, and the result-processing block was overwriting the accumulated transcript with "".

Fix: two guards in SpeechRecognizerService.
1. Skip the fullTranscript/transcript mutation when the callback's formattedString is empty (cumulative results from a live recognizer are never empty — only teardown produces "").
2. In stopListening(), snapshot fullTranscript before canceling the task and restore it after teardown, so any late callback that beats us cannot wipe a completed session.

No other behavior changes.
