Build 101 — Auto-resume after declined calls

THE ONE MISSING PIECE FROM BUILD 100 IS FIXED.

Build 100 correctly detected that audio didn't restart after a declined phone call and showed the red alarm banner — but on many devices iOS takes 4-8 seconds to release the audio session after a declined call, longer than Build 100's 3 retries would wait. Build 101 fixes that.

• FIXED: Post-call retry ladder extended from 3 attempts (0.8s/1.5s/2.5s = 4.8s) to 5 attempts (1s/2s/4s/7s/12s = 26s). This covers even worst-case declined-call teardowns on WiFi Calling and Do Not Disturb.
• NEW: Background auto-retry loop. When the RECORDING STOPPED alarm fires, Ora now silently keeps trying to restart the audio engine every 3 seconds for up to 60 seconds. The moment audio buffers flow again, the banner dismisses itself, you get a success haptic, and recording continues — no button tap required.
• UPDATED: Alarm banner now says "Trying to resume automatically..." with a spinner while the background loop is running. The SAVE and RETRY buttons remain available for manual override.
• Same behavior applies to the every-second health check: if audio dies unexpectedly (not just from a call), Ora will try to recover in the background before giving up.
• All Build 100 fixes are still in place: transcript survives interruptions, share audio button, recording-health monitoring, transcript recovery on relaunch.
