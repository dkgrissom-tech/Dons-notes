Build 104 — Big fix + big feature. This one changes everything.

FIXED: The banner-flicker bug. When the audio engine died, an automatic retry loop was fighting itself — banner on, banner off, banner on, over and over. It's gone. Recording now behaves predictably.

NEW: A big CONTINUE RECORDING button lives on the main recording screen at all times. Tap it any time you suspect something's off — the app instantly force-restarts the mic. No hunting through menus, no waiting for the alarm banner.
  • When everything is healthy, the button is subtle gray so it doesn't distract.
  • When audio has stopped, the button turns bold red so you can't miss it.

NEW: Background audio. Ora now keeps recording when you check your Calendar, open Messages, look at Slack, or lock your screen. The iOS red mic pill stays visible in the status bar so you always know recording is live. This is the same permission voice memo apps use — meetings survive the moment you glance at another app.

CHANGED: The alarm banner. When the mic dies, the banner appears once and stays put until you tap CONTINUE RECORDING or SAVE. No more flicker cycle. Diagnostic log is still one tap away for post-mortem analysis (Build 103 feature).

Carries forward: transcript survives interruptions, share audio button, transcript recovery, forceRestart zombie-engine recovery, diagnostic logging with COPY LOG and long-press-orb log viewer.
