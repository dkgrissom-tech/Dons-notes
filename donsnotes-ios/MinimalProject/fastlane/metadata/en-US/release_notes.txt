Build 103 — Diagnostic logging for post-mortem analysis

Adds a full instrumentation layer so the next time a recording fails, we can see exactly which step tripped instead of guessing.

• NEW: In-memory diagnostic log captures every recording-lifecycle event with millisecond timestamps — call state transitions, audio session activate/deactivate, engine start/stop, tap install, first buffer received, retry ladder attempts, alarm triggers, RETRY/SAVE button taps, forceRestart step-by-step trace.

• NEW: When the red RECORDING STOPPED alarm banner appears, a third button "COPY DIAGNOSTIC LOG" now sits under SAVE/RETRY. Tap it to copy the full log to the iPhone clipboard, then paste it into Notes/Messages/Mail and send it back for analysis.

• NEW: Long-press the recording orb for 3 seconds any time (even during a healthy recording) to open a full-screen diagnostic log viewer. Includes a Share button that hands the log off to AirDrop, Mail, Messages, or iCloud Drive.

• Log is capped at 500 entries with a ring buffer so long meetings don't grow unbounded. Zero performance impact on the hot audio callback path.

• Carries forward all Build 100-102 fixes: transcript survives interruptions, share audio button, recording-health alarm with red banner, transcript recovery on relaunch, forceRestart zombie-engine recovery, extended retry ladder, background auto-retry with spinner and success haptic.
